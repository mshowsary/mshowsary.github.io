Christmas Glow Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Christmas Glow (package com.glowcolor.christmas), a stained-glass color-by-number game, handles information. "We" means the developer of Christmas Glow, as named on the app's Google Play page.

  • No account, no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or any other personal data.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Pictures, settings and streaks are saved only on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. The ads software collects some device and usage data (described below). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and in US states with privacy laws you can opt out.
  • No ads while you color. Rewarded videos play only when you choose to watch one.

1. Information we do not collect

Christmas Glow has no user accounts and no sign-in. The app does not send your game data to us, and we do not run servers that receive personal information from the app. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services. We never see what you color, the pictures you finish or the cards you make.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device so you can pick up where you left off: which pieces you have filled, finished pictures and their replays, unlocked pictures, advent doors you have opened, your daily gift streak, hints and your settings (sound, music, vibration, reminders). This information stays in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Christmas Glow is free and is funded by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and Switzerland). How ads appear in the app:

  • Rewarded videos only when you choose to watch one, for example for a hint, a bonus picture, opening an advent door early or doubling the daily gift.
  • An occasional full-screen ad after you finish a picture, never while you are coloring.
  • A banner in the picture library only.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app collects and shares the following with Google:

DataExamples
Device and advertising identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID
Approximate locationGeneral location estimated from your IP address (not GPS)

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse and, where you allow it, show personalized ads. The data is encrypted in transit. The app asks Google for ads suitable for a general audience (maximum ad content rating "Teen").

Your consent and choices.

  • EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before any ads load, the app shows Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform, an IAB TCF certified consent tool). You can consent, decline, or choose individual purposes. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalized or "limited" ads, which use less data.
  • United States: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Texas), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising through the same Google message.
  • Where the law requires it, the app's settings include an Ad privacy choices button so you can review or change your choice at any time.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). If you delete it, apps can no longer use it for personalized ads.

4. Notifications

If you turn them on, the app can remind you about your daily gift or a new advent door. These reminders are scheduled on your device by the app itself; no notification server is involved and no data is sent anywhere. Android asks for your permission first, and you can turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings at any time.

5. Sharing your art

When you tap Share (for example to send a Christmas card), the app creates the image on your device and opens Android's share sheet. Only the picture and message you choose are passed to the app you select (such as a messaging or email app), and that app's own privacy policy applies from then on. We do not receive a copy.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads. Coloring itself works offline.
  • Notifications: optional reminders (see section 4).
  • Vibration: gentle haptic feedback while coloring.
  • Run at startup and keep awake: only to restore your reminders after the phone restarts and deliver them on time.
  • Advertising ID and ad services: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described in section 3.

The app does not request access to your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

7. Children

Christmas Glow is designed for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing on our side to delete; if you believe a child has used the app, uninstalling it removes all of its data from the device, and the device's advertising ID can be reset or deleted in Android settings. You are welcome to contact us with any concern.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data: kept only on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Settings > Apps > Christmas Glow > Storage > Clear storage).
  • Advertising data: held by Google under its own retention rules (see the links in section 3). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings and manage Google ad settings at My Ad Center.
  • There is no account, so there is no account to delete and no deletion request is needed.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent and to opt out of targeted advertising. We do not hold personal data about you, so most requests are best handled through the controls above or directly with Google. If you have a question or want to exercise a right, contact us and we will help. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security and international transfers

Data collected by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google may process it in countries other than your own, under the safeguards described in Google's privacy policy.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's Google Play release notes.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: support@playreadytv.net
